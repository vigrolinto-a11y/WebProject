About Our Birdwatching Project

This project was created to showcase the diverse bird species found in Botswana. Our mission is to promote conservation, raise awareness, and inspire appreciation for the country’s rich avian life.

Whether you're a beginner or an expert birder, our website helps you discover new species, explore habitats, and learn the best times to watch birds in Botswana.
